## Understanding Depression

Depression is a common mental health disorder that affects mood, thoughts, energy levels, and daily functioning.

Unlike a simple period of sadness, depression persists over time and can have a significant impact on personal, family, professional, and social life.

It can affect anyone, regardless of age or life situation. Fortunately, depression can be effectively treated with an approach tailored to each individual.

At **Clinique Santé Mentale en Ligne**, we offer mental health assessments via teleconsultation to help people better understand their symptoms and receive personalized recommendations.

# What are the symptoms of depression?

Symptoms can vary from person to person. The most common ones include:

### Symptômes physiques et cognitifs

- fatigue importante;
- manque d’énergie;
- troubles du sommeil;
- changements de l’appétit;
- difficultés de concentration;
- ralentissement de la pensée;
- diminution de la motivation.

### Behavioral Changes

- social isolation;
- discontinuation of usual activities;
- a decline in performance at work or in school;
- difficulty performing daily tasks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Is depression a sign of weakness ?

No. Depression is a medical condition that can affect anyone.

#### Can depression be cured ?

Yes. Most people show improvement with appropriate treatment.

#### Are antidepressants addictive ?

No. Antidepressants are generally not addictive, but they should be taken and discontinued according to a healthcare professional’s recommendations.

#### How long does it take to get better ?

The extent of improvement varies from person to person. With many treatments, it takes a few weeks before significant changes become apparent.

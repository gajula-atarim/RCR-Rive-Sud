Introduction

“His clinical and teaching experience enables him to provide thorough instruction focused on practical skills and tailored to participants’ needs.”

Fethi Boudebza is an instructor certified by the Heart and Stroke Foundation of Canada. Certified since 2017, he has several years of experience in cardiopulmonary resuscitation and clinical training.

He has served as the Code Blue team leader at Douglas Hospital. He has also trained staff in CPR, first aid and responding to life-threatening emergencies. In addition, he provided CPR training to the nursing staff at the Philippe-Pinel Institute of Montreal.

As a specialised nurse practitioner (SNP), he also has clinical experience in acute care at the Jewish General Hospital. He has also taught nursing at Cégep André-Laurendeau.

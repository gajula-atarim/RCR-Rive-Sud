Cardiac First Aid

CPR, Automated External Defibrillator (AED) and Choking Response. Heart & Stroke’s Cardiac First Aid course teaches you how to recognise cardiac arrest and respond quickly using cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), an automated external defibrillator (AED) and techniques to clear a blocked airway.

Overview

Become a vital link in the chain of survival. No medical background is required. This course is for people who want to gain the essential skills needed to respond to emergencies, both at work and in everyday life.

Choose the level that meets your needs

Level A — Adult

Approximate classroom duration: 2 hours
  • CPR and emergency response for adults
  • Workplaces where emergencies mainly involve adults
  • Offices, retail businesses and certain industrial environments

Level C — Adult, Child and Infant

Approximate classroom duration: 3 hours 30 minutes
  • Emergency response for people of all ages
  • Parents, childcare and school staff, sports coaches
  • Healthcare professionals who want to develop paediatric CPR skills

Certification

A Heart & Stroke course completion card is awarded to participants who meet the programme requirements. The stated validity period is two years, subject to the conditions applicable to the relevant certification.
